# Sample Road Scene Assets

This folder contains sample test assets for demonstrating the VisionHack ADAS pipeline.

### Road Scene Samples
- `rainy_night_city.png`: Real-world urban driving capture on a rainy night featuring wet asphalt reflections, traffic taillights, headlights, motorcycles, and passenger cars.
- `road_day_normal.jpg`: Daylight road scene benchmark.
- `road_night_dark.jpg`: Underexposed nighttime road scene.
- `road_foggy_simulated.jpg`: Atmospheric scattering / Koschmieder fog simulation scene.
- `demo_road_clip.mp4`: Short multi-frame video sequence for temporal tracking and batch video analysis.
